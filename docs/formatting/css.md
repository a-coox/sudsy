# CSS / SCSS / Less (`lang-css`)

Three languages, `css`, `scss` and `less`, in one crate: one grammar (`grammars/css.grammar`), one lexer and parser, three providers. `scss` and `less` use `css` as their config base (`config_base()`): a `css:` section applies to all three and `scss:`/`less:` override it. Extensions `.css`, `.scss`, `.less`. The Sass indented syntax (`.sass`) is not supported. The plan for the rest is [../design/wave-3b-css.md](../design/wave-3b-css.md).

## Parser
Hand-written lexer and recursive-descent parser, lossless: every byte is in a token and trivia are placed by the shared attachment rule.
- **Lexer:** CSS Syntax Level 3 tokens (identifiers with escapes, at-keywords, hashes, strings, numbers, percentages, dimensions, `url(` with an unquoted body, unicode ranges). A sign directly after an identifier or number is a delimiter (`n+3` and `n + 3` are the same tokens). Per dialect: `//` comments (SCSS, Less), `$name` and `#{` (SCSS), `@{` (Less). An unterminated string, comment or `url(` is an `Unknown` token and an error. A `url(` whose body is quoted, a call or an interpolation is an ordinary function.
- **Parser:** a statement is told apart by what ends it, found by a scan that balances `()`, `[]` and `#{}` (braces too inside a custom property's value): `{` makes a `Rule`; `;` or `}` a `Declaration` if a `:` came first outside any group, else a `Statement`; a statement that starts with an at-keyword is an `AtRule` (so `@if`, `@mixin`, Less `@var: value`, detached rulesets and mixin definitions with guards need no rules of their own). An `@else` right after the block of an `@if` or `@else` (no comment between) is nested in that at-rule as its last child, so a chain is one node; each link counts as a level of nesting. In the parameters of `@include`, `@mixin` and `@function` the first name followed by `(` is a `Function` even with blanks before the `(`. Preludes, properties and values are `Prelude`, `Property` and `Value` nodes of tokens, `Function`s (a name directly followed by `(`) and `Group`s (`()`, `[]`, `#{}`, and `{}` in a group). Dialect constructs that formatting or rules need get their own nodes in later steps; until then a Less mixin call `.m(1);` is a `Statement` and `.a:extend(.b);` a `Declaration`.
- **Errors:** per statement. An unmatched `)`, `]` or a `}` inside a group ends the statement as an `ERROR` node, an unclosed group makes the rest of the file one, a stray top-level `}` is one token in an `ERROR` node, a missing `}` is one diagnostic. Later statements still parse. Nesting of blocks or groups beyond 256 is an error (the rest of the file is one `ERROR` node; bounds recursion in parser, formatter and drop).

## Formatting
Prettier's output (printer-postcss) is the reference; defaults equal Prettier's. The tree is printed through the shared block and statement-sequence shapes (one statement per line, blank lines kept up to `max_blank_lines`, `prettier-ignore` and `sudsy-ignore` before a statement or `sudsy-ignore-start`/`-end`); prelude, property and value nodes become flat lists of items (tokens, functions, groups) that the printers walk. `lang-css/src/format/`: `mod.rs` (statements, blocks, at-rules, declarations), `selector.rs`, `value.rs` (values and at-rule parameters), `text.rs` (numbers, strings, colors).
- **Rules and blocks:** `selector {`, one declaration per line, `}`; an empty block is `{` and `}` on two lines. A comment before a statement goes on its own line; one after `;` or `{` on its line stays there (after `{` it becomes the first line of the block). A stray `;` between statements is dropped unless it carries a comment (then it stays with the comment), a declaration without `;` gets one.
- **Selectors:** one per line; `>`, `+`, `~` have single spaces around them; other whitespace is one space; a selector longer than the width breaks before its combinators and spaces (indented). Attribute selectors `[a ~= b i]` become `[a~="b" i]`; pseudo-classes are lowercase (not `:--custom`), `:not()`, `:is()`, `:where()` and the like hold a selector list (`, ` or one per line when too long), `:nth-*()` is `2n + 1`; in `@keyframes` `FROM` and `TO` are lowercase. A selector with a construct not known here (mixin definitions and guards, `&` with a function) is kept as written, in a single line with its strings in the preferred quote when it has no comment or line break.
- **Declarations:** lowercase property (kept for custom properties, `$var`, interpolation, and inside `:import` and `:export`), `prop: value;`, `!important` (and `!default`, `!global`) lowercase after a space. Values: words separated by one space where the source had whitespace, adjacent ones joined (`1px/2px`, `a,b` is `a, b`), `*` always spaced (`/` only in `calc()` and when already spaced). A comma list that does not fit starts on its own line and wraps like a fill; one with a multi-word item (`transition: opacity 0.3s ease, transform 0.3s`) always goes one item per line; arguments of a function that does not fit go one per line. Numbers: lowercase `e`, no trailing zeros, a digit before the dot (`+.50em` is `+0.5em`); units in their canonical case (`PX` is `px`, `hz` is `Hz`); hex colors and CSS-wide keywords lowercase; `url()` as written. Strings use the preferred quote (`single_quote`) unless they hold more of it, and a string that has its quote is untouched. `grid`, `grid-template*` and `grid-area` keep the line breaks of their value; `composes` is one line.
- **At-rules:** lowercase name; SCSS `@if`, `@else`, `@each`, `@for`, `@while`, `@return`, `@debug`, `@warn`, `@error`, `@use`, `@forward`, `@extend`, `@at-root`, `@include`, `@mixin` and `@function` print their parameters as values (`@include`, `@mixin` and `@function` break their arguments one per line when too long); `} @else` stays on the line of the `}`; a control directive's parameters break at every space or at none (`==`, `!=`, `<`, `>`, `and`, `or`, `not` stay with the word before, `in`, `from`, `through` and `end` with the word after) and then the `{` goes on its own line, except after a parenthesis; `@use ... with (...)` always breaks; `@media`/`@custom-media` parameters get `: ` in parentheses, only their commas break; `@supports`, `@container`, `@import`, `@layer`, `@page`, `@font-face`, `@keyframes`, `@namespace`, `@property`, `@counter-style`, `@scope` and `@starting-style` are printed as values; `@charset` and every other at-rule (Less mixins and control directives) keep their parameters as written on one line (strings in the preferred quote). A Less `@var: value;` is printed like a declaration and a detached ruleset `@var: { }` ends with `;`.
- **Comments:** inside a value or at-rule parameter a block comment is a word; a line comment inside a statement, or any comment inside a selector or between property and value keeps the statement as written.
- **Safety check:** the formatter rewrites the text of identifiers, at-keywords, numbers, dimensions, hashes, strings and `url()` (tagged `#restyle`), and a missing `;` is added: `CssFormatter::extra_check` compares the tokens of input and output with case, number spelling, quote style and the blanks of `url()` normalized.
- **SCSS values:** a map `(key: value, ...)` always breaks, one entry per line with a trailing comma, in a `$variable`, as an argument of a function or inside another map (also a list that is the value of an entry); a trailing comma of any parenthesis is dropped; a blank line after a comma inside parentheses stays; `$a/2` is one word, a `/` is spaced when the source spaces it, after a function or parenthesis and between a number and a variable, and `/` in `calc()` always; `+` next to a variable, function or parenthesis is spaced; operators next to `#{}` stay as written; a Less lookup `@map[key]` has no space before `[`; a custom property of blanks only keeps them, and one whose whole value is a `{ }` block (`--x: { color: red }`, parsed as a `Block` in the `Value`) prints it as a block ending in `;`.
- **Not laid out yet:** Less mixin call arguments and detached ruleset arguments (`.m({...})`, `each(@l, {...})`), mixin guards, long `:extend()` lists, `@nest` and `@custom-selector` selectors, a front matter block, `1.` style numbers, signs that Prettier joins to the next number, math inside `url()`, comments inside values, selectors and maps.

## Rules
The tree-only core rules of stylelint, with stylelint's ids, in `lang-css/rules/*.yaml` for the three languages; all are off by default and the presets `css/stylelint-recommended` and `css/stylelint-standard` ([presets.md](presets.md)) turn them on. The catalog is [../parity/stylelint.yaml](../parity/stylelint.yaml); `scripts/parity.sh stylelint` compares them with stylelint on `tests/rules/css`. A rule is one of:
- a pattern file over the tree (`block-no-empty`, `comment-no-empty`, `no-empty-source`, `declaration-no-important`, `keyframe-declaration-no-important`, `color-no-invalid-hex`, `length-zero-no-unit`, `function-url-quotes`, `import-notation`, `no-invalid-double-slash-comments`, `selector-pseudo-element-colon-notation`, `selector-attribute-quotes`, `comment-whitespace-inside`);
- a row of the family `names` (`src/rules/names.rs`): the names of properties, units, functions, at-rules, media features, selector parts (class, id, attribute, pseudo-class, pseudo-element, type, combinator), font families, keyframes, custom properties and custom media, checked as `allowed` or `disallowed` (option `list`: names or `/regex/`), `pattern` (option `pattern`), `lower` (case) or `unique`;
- a row of the family `max` (`max.rs`): a count per selector (`class`, `id`, `attribute`, `pseudo-class`, `type`, `universal`, `combinators`, `compounds`), the `nesting-depth`, the `declarations` of a single-line block and the `decimals` of a number, against option `max`;
- a row of the family `check` (`checks.rs`, `values.rs`): rules that read the tree their own way (duplicates, `@import` position, `calc()` operators, generic font families, shorthand overrides, descending specificity, declaration position, grid areas, hex length, redundant values, ...).

The model (`model.rs`) gives the rules the property and at-rule names, the selectors of a rule split into compounds and parts (a functional pseudo-class adds what its arguments hold to its compound), and the tokens of a value. Names are compared as stylelint does: properties, at-rules, units and pseudo-classes in lowercase, classes, ids, functions and font families as written; the `pattern` of a custom property or custom media is matched against the name without `--`. Custom properties in `var()` count for `custom-property-pattern`. Not built: the rules that need data tables (`*-no-unknown`, `*-no-vendor-prefix`, `*-no-deprecated`), those whose options are maps (`declaration-property-unit-allowed-list`, ...), the `*-empty-line-before` family, the notation rules over color functions and `string-no-newline` (the lexer reports an unterminated string as a syntax error).

## Conformance
The real projects of `tests/conformance/fetch-css-corpus.sh` (Bootstrap, Bulma, ant-design, Semantic UI, normalize.css, docusaurus; its Prettier fixtures belong to the parity harness and hold deliberate errors): every file parses without errors and formats with the shared checks (round trip, meaning, idempotency, comments kept). `cargo test --profile corpus -p lang-css --test css -- --ignored --nocapture conformance` after `bash tests/conformance/fetch-css-corpus.sh`.
