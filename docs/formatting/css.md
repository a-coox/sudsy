# CSS / SCSS / Less (`lang-css`)

Three languages, `css`, `scss` and `less`, in one crate: one grammar (`grammars/css.grammar`), one lexer and parser, three providers. `scss` and `less` use `css` as their config base (`config_base()`): a `css:` section applies to all three and `scss:`/`less:` override it. Extensions `.css`, `.scss`, `.less`. The Sass indented syntax (`.sass`) is not supported. The plan for the rest is [../design/wave-3b-css.md](../design/wave-3b-css.md).

## Parser
Hand-written lexer and recursive-descent parser, lossless: every byte is in a token and trivia are placed by the shared attachment rule.
- **Lexer:** CSS Syntax Level 3 tokens (identifiers with escapes, at-keywords, hashes, strings, numbers, percentages, dimensions, `url(` with an unquoted body, unicode ranges). A sign directly after an identifier or number is a delimiter (`n+3` and `n + 3` are the same tokens). Per dialect: `//` comments (SCSS, Less), `$name` and `#{` (SCSS), `@{` (Less). An unterminated string, comment or `url(` is an `Unknown` token and an error. A `url(` whose body is quoted, a call or an interpolation is an ordinary function.
- **Parser:** a statement is told apart by what ends it, found by a scan that balances `()`, `[]` and `#{}` (braces too inside a custom property's value): `{` makes a `Rule`; `;` or `}` a `Declaration` if a `:` came first outside any group, else a `Statement`; a statement that starts with an at-keyword is an `AtRule` (so `@if`, `@mixin`, Less `@var: value`, detached rulesets and mixin definitions with guards need no rules of their own). Preludes, properties and values are `Prelude`, `Property` and `Value` nodes of tokens, `Function`s (a name directly followed by `(`) and `Group`s (`()`, `[]`, `#{}`, and `{}` in a group). Dialect constructs that formatting or rules need get their own nodes in later steps; until then a Less mixin call `.m(1);` is a `Statement` and `.a:extend(.b);` a `Declaration`.
- **Errors:** per statement. An unmatched `)`, `]` or a `}` inside a group ends the statement as an `ERROR` node, an unclosed group makes the rest of the file one, a stray top-level `}` is one token in an `ERROR` node, a missing `}` is one diagnostic. Later statements still parse. Nesting of blocks or groups beyond 256 is an error (the rest of the file is one `ERROR` node; bounds recursion in parser, formatter and drop).

## Formatting
Prettier's output (printer-postcss) is the reference; defaults equal Prettier's. The tree is printed through the shared block and statement-sequence shapes (one statement per line, blank lines kept up to `max_blank_lines`, `prettier-ignore` and `sudsy-ignore` before a statement or `sudsy-ignore-start`/`-end`); prelude, property and value nodes become flat lists of items (tokens, functions, groups) that the printers walk. `lang-css/src/format/`: `mod.rs` (statements, blocks, at-rules, declarations), `selector.rs`, `value.rs` (values and at-rule parameters), `text.rs` (numbers, strings, colors).
- **Rules and blocks:** `selector {`, one declaration per line, `}`; an empty block is `{` and `}` on two lines. A comment before a statement goes on its own line; one after `;` or `{` on its line stays there (after `{` it becomes the first line of the block). A stray `;` between statements is dropped unless it carries a comment (then it stays with the comment), a declaration without `;` gets one.
- **Selectors:** one per line; `>`, `+`, `~` have single spaces around them; other whitespace is one space; a selector longer than the width breaks before its combinators and spaces (indented). Attribute selectors `[a ~= b i]` become `[a~="b" i]`; pseudo-classes are lowercase (not `:--custom`), `:not()`, `:is()`, `:where()` and the like hold a selector list (`, ` or one per line when too long), `:nth-*()` is `2n + 1`; in `@keyframes` `FROM` and `TO` are lowercase. A selector with a construct not known here (mixin definitions and guards, `&` with a function) is kept as written, in a single line with its strings in the preferred quote when it has no comment or line break.
- **Declarations:** lowercase property (kept for custom properties, `$var`, interpolation, and inside `:import` and `:export`), `prop: value;`, `!important` (and `!default`, `!global`) lowercase after a space. Values: words separated by one space where the source had whitespace, adjacent ones joined (`1px/2px`, `a,b` is `a, b`), `*` always spaced (`/` only in `calc()` and when already spaced). A comma list that does not fit starts on its own line and wraps like a fill; one with a multi-word item (`transition: opacity 0.3s ease, transform 0.3s`) always goes one item per line; arguments of a function that does not fit go one per line. Numbers: lowercase `e`, no trailing zeros, a digit before the dot (`+.50em` is `+0.5em`); units in their canonical case (`PX` is `px`, `hz` is `Hz`); hex colors and CSS-wide keywords lowercase; `url()` as written. Strings use the preferred quote (`single_quote`) unless they hold more of it, and a string that has its quote is untouched. `grid`, `grid-template*` and `grid-area` keep the line breaks of their value; `composes` is one line.
- **At-rules:** lowercase name; `@media`/`@custom-media` parameters get `: ` in parentheses, only their commas break; `@supports`, `@container`, `@import`, `@layer`, `@page`, `@font-face`, `@keyframes`, `@namespace`, `@property`, `@counter-style`, `@scope` and `@starting-style` are printed as values; `@charset` and every other at-rule (SCSS and Less: `@include`, `@mixin`, `@if`, `@each`, Less mixins) keep their parameters as written on one line (strings in the preferred quote). A Less `@var: value;` is printed like a declaration.
- **Comments:** inside a value or at-rule parameter a block comment is a word; a line comment inside a statement, or any comment inside a selector or between property and value keeps the statement as written.
- **Safety check:** the formatter rewrites the text of identifiers, at-keywords, numbers, dimensions, hashes, strings and `url()` (tagged `#restyle`), and a missing `;` is added: `CssFormatter::extra_check` compares the tokens of input and output with case, number spelling, quote style and the blanks of `url()` normalized.
- **Not laid out yet:** SCSS maps and `@include`/`@mixin`/`@function` arguments, `@else` after `}` (it starts its own line), control-flow expressions, Less mixin call arguments and detached ruleset calls, `@nest` and `@custom-selector` selectors, a front matter block, brace values of custom properties and `1.` style numbers.

## Conformance
The real projects of `tests/conformance/fetch-css-corpus.sh` (Bootstrap, Bulma, ant-design, Semantic UI, normalize.css, docusaurus; its Prettier fixtures belong to the parity harness and hold deliberate errors): every file parses without errors and formats with the shared checks (round trip, meaning, idempotency, comments kept). `cargo test --profile corpus -p lang-css --test css -- --ignored --nocapture conformance` after `bash tests/conformance/fetch-css-corpus.sh`.
